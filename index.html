Vantablack

Every message leaves by three roads at once, and no machine on any of them holds the whole thing.

The problem

A VPN provider sees every site you visit, keeps logs, and can be ordered to hand them over. Separately, traffic recorded today can be decrypted later if quantum computers get strong enough.

How it works

Vantablack connects your machine to other machines and uses them to carry your traffic. Each message is encrypted with hybrid ML-KEM-768 and X25519, split into three Reed–Solomon shards, and sent along divergent routes. A shard that arrives modified fails its own authentication tag and is discarded before reassembly.

What it gives you

  • No provider. There is nobody holding your traffic to log it.
  • Post-quantum encryption. ML-KEM-768 with X25519, so a recording made today has to be broken against a cipher built for later.
  • Tamper detection. Every shard carries its own HKDF-derived key and Poly1305 tag.
  • Packet-loss tolerance. Any two of the three shards rebuild the message.

Setup

1Install the Windows installer, or build with cargo
2Point it at a peer, or let it discover one over Cloudflare
3Route traffic through the mesh SOCKS5 proxy
One binary. You run a node.

Limits

Running it means running a node. There is no support and no company responsible for your uptime. The seven-node Level 2 carrier mesh is a simulation, and the physical-anchor modules ship disabled by default.

HoloForge

It prints the whole object at once, in the time a normal printer spends on its first hundred layers.

The problem

Layer-by-layer resin printers run for hours, leave stair-stepping striations where one layer meets the next, and print supports that have to be cut off afterwards.

How it works

HoloForge computes holograms that make 405 nm light from several rotary angles intersect only where the part should cure, then models what follows: radical polymerisation, dissolved-oxygen inhibition, and the surface the finished part measures.

What it gives you

  • Under fifteen seconds per part, with no support structures to remove.
  • Cure accuracy of 94.6–96.7% IoU in simulation, with under 0.02% overcure.
  • Parametric generators for dental crowns, microfluidic chips and aspheric lenses, plus arbitrary .stl voxelisation.
  • A vat aberration conjugate mask that cancels cylindrical vial astigmatism on the SLM, without an index-matching bath.

Setup

1py -m pip install -e .
2Load an .stl and synthesise the holograms
3Run a simulated rotary print against the digital twin
Python only. No hardware.

Limits

A simulated print is not a physical print. The optical and photochemical models are physical; the machine is emulated, and real output depends on your laser, your resin and your vat.

MuonScope Tomography

Particles falling through your roof right now can map the inside of a concrete wall.

The problem

Industrial X-ray and gamma radiography cannot penetrate more than a few centimetres of dense matter and brings its own radiation hazard. Ground-penetrating radar attenuates in wet or conductive soil beyond two to five metres. Core drilling is destructive and runs to tens of thousands of euros per borehole.

How it works

Cosmic-ray muons arrive with a mean energy near 3–4 GeV and cross tens to hundreds of metres of rock, concrete and steel. MuonScope measures their scattering angles and flux attenuation across a multi-layer silicon-photomultiplier hodoscope with FPGA time-stamping, then inverts the deflections into a 3D density map.

What it gives you

  • No radiation source. The muons are already there.
  • Voids, density anomalies, reinforcement and heavy-element shielding resolved in three dimensions.
  • A backpack-portable station: under 9.5 kg per panel, drawing under 15 W.
  • Dual-band RTK-GNSS positioning with Bayesian inversion over the measured tracks.

Setup

1Build the two detector panels from the bill of materials
2Flash the FPGA coincidence and time-stamping firmware
3Run the Python inversion over the captured tracks
A hardware build, not a software install.

Limits

Positioning is the hard part: indoors, underground or under canopy there is no GNSS, so the station falls back on ultra-wideband, fiducial mounts or dead reckoning. Detector timing bin widths drift with temperature and the dark-count rate climbs in heat, both of which the risk register tracks as open engineering work.

PlaneOS

Before a command moves a control surface, the software has to prove it cannot put the aircraft into a lethal state.

The problem

Flight-envelope protection is normally enforced by test coverage and review. Neither can enumerate the states an aircraft can reach, so the assurance rests on the cases someone thought to try.

How it works

Every command is translated into a formula over the control-cycle state. Envelope, Mach, altitude and terrain hazards are expanded over a prediction horizon and checked symbolically on the current and predicted state. A command reaches the dynamics integrator only when every hazard formula is proven unsatisfiable; otherwise the aircraft freezes. Each verdict is written to a hash-chained flight-recorder specification.

What it gives you

  • A trustless execution gate. The control law is an untrusted candidate generator, so a software fault cannot bypass the proof.
  • Taint-coded sensor audit. Pitot, IMU, GPS, ADS-B and radar data are tracked to the channels they feed.
  • Fail-safe by construction. An unproven hazard stops the command. It is not recorded as a warning.
  • A machine-readable record. Every verdict lands in a hash-chained specification.

Setup

1cargo test — 63 passing
2Run the demo against the stub solver
3Enable the z3 feature for real non-linear arithmetic proofs
A Rust crate. No hardware.

Limits

The flight dynamics are a mock model, the recorder hashes with FNV-1a, and there is no real-time scheduling, hardware-in-the-loop testing or actuator-attack modelling in the prototype. It is a research prototype, not deployable, and not certified to DO-178C, CS-25 or ARP4754A.

SybilGuard

Prove you are one real person without saying which person.

The problem

One person can hold ten thousand accounts, which breaks anything that counts people: votes, reputation, giveaways, benefits. The usual repair is to demand identity documents, and then every service ends up holding a copy of your passport.

How it works

The proof is produced inside the security chip your device already carries, a Secure Enclave or a TPM, and is bound to a key that cannot be extracted. You publish a proof that a unique human holds that key, together with a nullifier derived from the service identifier, so a service can see that a person has not claimed yet without being able to match that claim to your activity anywhere else.

What it gives you

  • One claim per person per service, enforced by a nullifier derived from the identity secret and the scope.
  • Biometrics stay on the device. Nothing identifying is transmitted, so there is nothing to leak.
  • No persistent pseudonym. A proof in one service cannot be matched to a proof in another.
  • Sub-second verification, on-chain through a contract or off-chain through a Rust batch verifier.

Setup

1Add the Rust verification crate, or build the Circom circuits
2Bind an identity key inside a Secure Enclave or TPM
3Verify on-chain, or in an off-chain batch
Circuit tooling and hardware binding. Aimed at integrators.

Limits

The proof binds to a key held inside a Secure Enclave or TPM. The circuit cannot establish that the enclave is genuine, and it cannot tell whether one person has enrolled two devices.

Planetary Entropy Mining

The certificate is grounded in a window of noise nobody could have predicted.

The problem

Emission and offset certificates are issued by registries, so their audit trail is paperwork held by the issuer. A buyer has to trust the registry. The measurement cannot be checked.

How it works

A node captures a window of environmental noise, reduces it to a histogram and estimates its min-entropy with the NIST SP 800-90B estimators. A zero-knowledge circuit proves that the histogram meets a min-entropy threshold and that the node holds a matching hardening polynomial and nullifier — without revealing where the measuring node is. The certificate is then sharded, carried over an intermittent mesh, matched in a blind dark pool and settled once per nullifier.

What it gives you

  • Provenance from a measurement, not from an issuer's ledger.
  • Location-blind proofs. A verifier learns that the measurement was honest, and nothing about where it was taken.
  • A real min-entropy gate. The compiled circuit bounds the most common histogram bin. The earlier circuit checked a placeholder value.
  • Thirteen stages under test, from capture through Reed–Solomon sharding, DTN delivery and settlement.

Setup

1py -m pip install -e ".[dev]"
2Run py -m pytest for the fast suite, or -m "" for the slow NIST tier
3Run py -m cli.demo --quick for the end-to-end chain
Python and pip. No hardware.

Limits

The physical sources are seeded simulators and the settlement ledger runs in memory. The Groth16 circuit proves the histogram gate and the commitment structure; the Solidity verifier checks the public layout. The pairing maths is not verified, and the whitepaper lists both as the next engineering item.
