Vantablack

Every message leaves by three roads at once, and no machine on any of them holds the whole thing.

The problem

A VPN provider sees every site you visit, keeps logs, and can be ordered to hand them over. Separately, traffic recorded today can be decrypted later if quantum computers get strong enough.

How it works

Vantablack connects your machine to other machines and uses them to carry your traffic. Each message is encrypted with hybrid ML-KEM-768 and X25519, split into three Reed–Solomon shards, and sent along divergent routes. A shard that arrives modified fails its own authentication tag and is discarded before reassembly.

What it gives you

  • No provider. There is nobody holding your traffic to log it.
  • Post-quantum encryption. ML-KEM-768 with X25519, so a recording made today has to be broken against a cipher built for later.
  • Tamper detection. Every shard carries its own HKDF-derived key and Poly1305 tag.
  • Packet-loss tolerance. Any two of the three shards rebuild the message.

Setup

1Install the Windows installer, or build with cargo
2Point it at a peer, or let it discover one over Cloudflare
3Route traffic through the mesh SOCKS5 proxy
One binary. You run a node.

Limits

Running it means running a node. There is no support and no company responsible for your uptime. The seven-node Level 2 carrier mesh is a simulation, and the physical-anchor modules ship disabled by default.

BauLens

Deterministic German and EU building code compliance validation engine, checking architectural parameters before ground is broken.

The problem

Building regulations in Germany (BauGB, LBO) and European standards are intricate, distributed across state-specific ordinances, and traditionally validated through manual expert reviews. Errors caught late incur severe project delays and permit rejections.

How it works

BauLens parses IFC/BIM structural models and dimensional specs into an abstract regulatory syntax tree. Rule engines run deterministic geometric, fire safety, escape route width, and accessibility clearance checks against DIN and Eurocode criteria without cloud dependencies or black-box heuristics.

What it gives you

  • Zero-hallucination compliance. Pure deterministic rule execution against authoritative state building ordinances.
  • IFC & BIM integration. Direct spatial parsing of architectural geometry, egress corridors, and stair pitch tolerances.
  • Instant permit pre-check. Generates audit-ready compliance dossiers with exact legal citations.
  • Local air-gapped runtime. All calculation happens on-device with zero exposure of confidential architectural blueprints.

Setup

1Clone repository or launch the web runtime
2Load an .ifc architectural model or dimensional JSON
3Run the compliance verification suite against targeted state LBO
Pure rules engine. No hardware.

Limits

Validates geometric, structural, and dimensional criteria; legal interpretations of discretionary zoning clauses still require licensed surveyor sign-off.

ClauseLens

Automated contract clause contradiction and risk scoring engine grounded in formal logic.

The problem

Commercial agreements hide conflicting indemnities, ambiguous termination rights, and contradictory governing law clauses across hundreds of pages of schedules and amendments.

How it works

ClauseLens translates contractual commitments into deontic logic specifications (obligations, permissions, prohibitions). It then evaluates mutual consistency and liability caps across interdependent documents, surfacing direct logical contradictions and unmitigated exposure.

What it gives you

  • Automated contradiction detection. Identifies conflicting covenants, overlapping notice windows, and mutual exclusivity paradoxes.
  • Bounded risk scoring. Quantifies structural exposure across indemnity caps, force majeure, and dispute escalation trees.
  • Deontic logic graph. Visualizes dependency chains between conditioned obligations and payment milestones.
  • Client-side execution. Complete confidentiality — documents are analyzed locally in-memory without third-party LLM transmission.

Setup

1Clone repo or load web workspace
2Ingest bilateral agreement clauses or markdown contracts
3Execute formal consistency check and risk audit
Formal logic consistency. Client-side verified.

Limits

Evaluates logical contradictions and parameter boundaries; jurisdiction-specific common law case law nuances require human legal counsel.

Ambient Thermal Arbitrage

Virtual battery energy management system that turns building thermal mass into high-efficiency distributed grid storage.

The problem

Electrochemical batteries (lithium-ion) carry heavy capital costs, degradation cycles, and supply chain constraints. Meanwhile, existing heat pumps and HVAC systems consume energy reactively rather than leveraging dynamic day/night thermal capacitance.

How it works

Ambient Thermal Arbitrage calculates high-resolution thermodynamic drift models for physical building structures. By predicting ambient weather gradients and dynamic electricity spot prices, it pre-cools or pre-heats structural thermal mass within tight comfort deadbands, arbitraging power prices without electrochemical wear.

What it gives you

  • Zero chemical degradation. Exploits physical structural mass as a maintenance-free virtual storage battery.
  • Spot-market price arbitrage. Shifts megawatt-scale demand curves to negative or lowest-cost tariff windows.
  • Rigorous comfort envelopes. Guaranteed interior temperature adherence using bounded predictive control.
  • Rust embedded runtime. Low-power determinism suitable for direct edge deployment on building automation controllers.

Setup

1cargo test — verify thermodynamic solver
2Configure building heat capacitance and envelope U-values
3Connect dynamic spot price API and dispatch schedule generator
Thermodynamic mass control. Pure software and sensorics.

Limits

Effective thermal storage capacity depends on structural insulation and thermal mass; dynamic spot pricing requires smart metering infrastructure.

HoloForge

It prints the whole object at once, in the time a normal printer spends on its first hundred layers.

The problem

Layer-by-layer resin printers run for hours, leave stair-stepping striations where one layer meets the next, and print supports that have to be cut off afterwards.

How it works

HoloForge computes holograms that make 405 nm light from several rotary angles intersect only where the part should cure, then models what follows: radical polymerisation, dissolved-oxygen inhibition, and the surface the finished part measures.

What it gives you

  • Under fifteen seconds per part, with no support structures to remove.
  • Cure accuracy of 94.6–96.7% IoU in simulation, with under 0.02% overcure.
  • Parametric generators for dental crowns, microfluidic chips and aspheric lenses, plus arbitrary .stl voxelisation.
  • A vat aberration conjugate mask that cancels cylindrical vial astigmatism on the SLM, without an index-matching bath.

Setup

1py -m pip install -e .
2Load an .stl and synthesise the holograms
3Run a simulated rotary print against the digital twin
Python only. No hardware.

Limits

A simulated print is not a physical print. The optical and photochemical models are physical; the machine is emulated, and real output depends on your laser, your resin and your vat.
