Cryptography & ZKRust · Tokio · UDP

Vantablack

The first finished system, and the one everything else was built to make possible: a serverless peer-to-peer WAN mesh in pure Rust. Nodes open direct authenticated tunnels, pad every packet to one uniform size and randomise their timing, and split each payload into erasure shards that leave by different routes. Traffic shape gives a deep packet inspection appliance nothing to hold on to.

Key exchange
ML-KEM-512 (Kyber) + X25519 ECDH
Routing
erasure-sharded onion routes
Resilience
Reed–Solomon RS(2,1) across relays

Volumetric 3D printingPython · wave optics

HoloForge

A holographic tomographic volumetric additive manufacturing suite. Instead of drawing an object layer by layer, it shapes coherent 405 nm wavefronts through a cylindrical photopolymer vat from many rotary angles at once, curing the whole part in under fifteen seconds and with no supports. The optics, the photochemistry and the machine are all simulated, so it runs end to end with no hardware attached.

Optics
split-step angular spectrum, 4f telecentric filtering
Holography
double-phase synthesis, multi-angle tomography
Result
94.6–96.7% cure IoU, under 0.02% overcure

MuonScope Tomography

Cosmic-ray muon scattering and absorption tomography. Natural relativistic muons pass through a structure and scatter; inverting those deflections recovers the interior density distribution of an opaque, heavily shielded object without introducing a radiation source.

PlaneOS

A high-assurance flight control prototype exploring deterministic scheduling and envelope protection. Static memory guarantees remove allocation jitter and collection pauses from critical control loops, so worst-case timing is a property of the design rather than a hope.

06
Cryptography & ZKPython · circom · Solidity

Planetary Entropy Mining

Emission certificates grounded in physical measurement instead of an issuer's ledger. A node captures a window of environmental noise, reduces it to a histogram, and proves in zero knowledge that the window really carries the min-entropy it claims — and was measured when it says it was — without revealing where the node sits. The cryptographic core is real; the sources and the ledger are simulated, so the whole chain runs on a laptop.

Entropy
NIST SP 800-90B estimators, validated against truth
Proof
256-bin histogram, max-count gate for min-entropy
Commitment
GF(256) hardening polynomial, Poseidon nullifier
Open system Source

How the work gets made

Deterministic by default

Static memory, fixed schedules, no hidden allocation on a control path. If the timing cannot be reasoned about, it does not ship.

Verified, not asserted

Formal methods, SMT solvers, symbolic execution and reproducible builds. A claim without a check is a marketing line.

Own the whole stack

From the kernel to the domain surface, one engineering standard and no black boxes left in between.
