Distributed Systems

Global Ghost Net

A serverless WAN mesh network daemon in pure Rust that eliminates centralized VPN relays and trusted Certificate Authorities.

What to look out for in the code:
  • Hybrid Post-Quantum Key Exchange: Combines NIST ML-KEM-512 (Kyber) with ephemeral X25519 for forward-secret resilience against future quantum decryption.
  • Reed-Solomon Sharding: Packets are split into mathematical shards (RS 2,1) and routed across divergent peer paths so no single relay sees the complete payload.
  • Uniform packet sizing & jitter: Mitigates statistical timing and traffic-length fingerprinting.
Primary Stack Rust • Tokio • UDP
Architecture P2P Mesh / Reed-Solomon
Companion Repos FluidIP, Vantablack
Applied Physics & DSP

MuonScope Tomography

A non-destructive 3D structural imaging system that uses naturally occurring, relativistic cosmic-ray muons to reconstruct interior density maps of shielded or dense objects.

What to look out for in the code:
  • Point of Closest Approach (PoCA) Inversion: 3D ray-tracing algorithm calculating deflection angles of muon trajectories passing through material volumes.
  • Hardware Digital Twin: Silicon strip detector geometry modeled alongside FPGA coincidence triggering logic.
Primary Stack Python • TypeScript • NumPy
Domain Scattering Tomography / DSP
Companion Repos MuonScope-Engine

  • Triple Modular Redundancy (TMR): Voting algorithms for cross-checking accelerometer and gyroscopic telemetry.
Primary Stack Rust (no_std friendly)

SybilGuard & ZK Protocols

A zero-knowledge proof-of-personhood protocol designed to resist Sybil attacks through graph topology metrics without exposing biometric identity data.

What to look out for in the code:

  • Homomorphic & SMPC primitives: Complementary work in FHE-DB and SMPC-DP for computing directly on encrypted records.
Primary Stack Rust • Bellman • Arkworks
Domain ZK Proofs / Graph Theory
