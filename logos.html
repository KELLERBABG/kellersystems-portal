Identity system v2

Paper, ink, one signal.

The portal runs a light-first technical dossier: a bone-paper surface ramp, near-black ink for type and rules, and a single reserved orange signal. Nothing is rounded, everything is ruled, and the monospace voice carries the technical detail.

01 Mark

The ruled monogram

An ink field, an orange inner frame, and a squared K. The frame holds the mark together at favicon size; the monogram carries it at masthead size.

The mark never sits on a photograph, is never given a gradient, and is never stretched. Minimum digital size is 24 px; below that, use the monogram letter tile instead.

02 Palette

One neutral ramp, one signal

The orange is an action token, not a decorative one: primary buttons, active and hover states, key metrics, focus rings, and the marker square. Everything else is paper and ink.

Contrast: ink on paper is 18:1, muted on paper 5.4:1, accent text on paper 5.1:1, ink on signal 6.2:1. Adding a second hue is the one change that breaks this palette — it immediately reads as generic.

05 Rules

  • 01Every entrance animation has a prefers-reduced-motion branch that renders the final state immediately, and nothing above the fold starts hidden.
  • 02The auto-scrolling band is decorative and aria-hidden; it pauses on hover and carries a visible pause control.
  • 03The accent never carries information on its own — chips, counts, and states are all readable in plain ink.
  • 04Focus is a 3 px signal ring at 3 px offset, never removed, and inverted to paper on ink bands.
  • 05Body measure stays under 75 characters; headings are the only place large type is allowed to run wide.
