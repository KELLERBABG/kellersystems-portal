Writing rules

05 · Voice

The copy is held to these six rules. They exist because the failure mode is invisible: a page can be grammatically clean and still read as though a machine wrote it to sound impressive.

  • 01No contrast constructions. State the true thing instead of arguing it against a false one.
  • 02No sentence explaining why the previous sentence mattered. Cut from the dash onward and check what was lost.
  • 03Never call our own work honest, measured or real. Put the figure in and leave it.
  • 04No editorialising about which part is interesting. No "the point is", "the price of", "the real work".
  • 05No closing aphorism. End on the last fact.
  • 06Headings are nouns: Figures, Limits, Setup — not verdicts on their own section.

A hook is still allowed, and chapter ledes use one. The test that separates a hook from a tic: a hook names a thing, a number or a consequence, and a tic comments on the naming.
